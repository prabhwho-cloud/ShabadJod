# ShabadJod
Shabad Jod  (Word builder) game for young curious minds to start making their first Punjabi words

Link - https://prabhwho-cloud.github.io/ShabadJod/ShabadJod.html

ਸ਼ਬਦ ਬਣਾਓ – Punjabi Word Builder

A simple, free web game that helps young students learn to spell Punjabi (Gurmukhi) words. The student looks at a picture, then taps the jumbled letters to put the word together in the right order.

Made for classroom use. It is a single HTML file, so there is nothing to install and no server is needed.

Features
🖼️ Picture clues: every word has a picture (emoji) to look at.
🔤 Jumbled letters: students tap the tiles to fill the slots in order. Tap a filled slot to take a letter back.
📈 Gets harder as you go: words move from short to long and from simple to harder matras. Levels change every 5 words.
🎲 Different every time: the word order is shuffled on every play, so students don't see the same sequence.
✅ Choose your matras: the teacher or student picks which matras to practise. ਮੁਕਤਾ (no matra) is the default.
🌐 All matras option: one tick for every word, with mixed matras.
💡 Hint, reset and skip buttons, plus a score and progress bar.
📱 Works on phones, tablets and computers, and follows light/dark mode.

How words are chosen: a word appears only if every matra in it is ticked. For example, ਕੇਲਾ needs both ਲਾਂਵ and ਕੰਨਾ. A ਬਿੰਦੀ always sits with another vowel, so ticking ਬਿੰਦੀ shows all the ਬਿੰਦੀ words.

How to play
Open the page.
(Optional) Open ਮਾਤਰਾਵਾਂ ਚੁਣੋ, tick the matras you want, and press ਸ਼ੁਰੂ ਕਰੋ / Start.
Look at the picture and tap the letters in the right order.
Get it right to earn 10 points. Using a hint costs 2 points.
